# Generative AI Use Policy

Our general stance on generative AI models is that while they may be useful for mass-generation
of repatative work, they hinder and complicate creation of projects such as this, both in code generation
by overcomplicating the solutions and creating suboptimal code and in content, by creating soulless,
repetative and derivative text.

For the purposes of this documents generative AI tools include, but are not limited to:

- Large language models such as Google Gemini, Claude and OpenAI's ChatGPT
- Downstream translation services that depend on these models
- Downstream code generation harnesses such as Codex, Cursor and Claude Code

To summarize, we may use generative AI tools as sophisticated search engines, but at
the time of this document their use do not go above this.

Below is the detailed use policy for Generative AI tools:

## Design

We do not use Generative AI products such as Figma Make or Claude Design when creating designs or mockups,
at most our AI use is limited as a sophisticated search engine.

## Asset & Textual Data

No AI (that is, LLMs like Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT) was used in generating the textual and visual contents
of these pages, while data we source (such as third party images, base maps, or external links, etc.) may contain
AI generated content beyond our control (for instance, a page we linked may contain LLM Text), content published by
us, and that we have put forward as we have written it does not contain or is not edited by large language models.

Due to the fact that textual data, labels and titles are critical to this project we feel uncomfortable giving
control of our words to AI tools, and as such our use of AI during content authorship is limited to using it to find
resources or preliminary research.

## Code Generation

As this is a learning project, our use of AI in code generation is severly limited, we use AI tools like
ChatGPT and Claude Code to debug specific problems and bugs, or to find issues in code.

AI Tools are sometimes used to generate small amounts of code, as of May 20, 2026, when these blocks are committed,
they are annotated with a `Assisted-by` trailer in `git` to keep track of AI generated code.

Among three categories this is the category where AI is used the most by far, and even then it is quite limited.

## Evolution of This Document

We may see evolutions to this policy, notably we are open to using more AI in code generation, such as refactoring
if we see significant gains in AI model prowess. However we are commited to retaining the content of the pages
completely 100% human written, as we believe a project like this, and more or less any meaningful project, requires
human intent with words to suceed.
